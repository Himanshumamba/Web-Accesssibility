Privacy Policy

I respect your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled.

Information Collected

When you request a free review or contact me about a project, I may receive your name, work email, agency or company name, website URL, service selection, and project details. Website analytics may also collect information about page views and interactions.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used to reply to your request, prepare a project quote, provide accessibility services, and understand how people use this website.

Service Providers

Website forms are processed by FormSubmit, which forwards form details to my email. Appointment booking is handled by Calendly. Google Analytics measures website visits and selected interactions. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. I do not sell your contact details.

External Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to external services such as Upwork and LinkedIn. When you visit those services, their own privacy policies apply.

Data Security

I take reasonable steps to protect information sent to me and keep it only as long as needed to respond to requests, provide services, and maintain business records.

Questions or Requests
