What a Shopify Accessibility Audit Can Cover

The review is scoped around your theme, store setup, and key customer journeys. Common areas include:

Theme and Navigation

Semantic structure, headings, keyboard access, visible focus, menus, dialogs, and responsive navigation in the agreed theme templates.

Products and Forms

Product information, image alternatives, variant selectors, quantity controls, labels, validation, and other controls customers use to choose products.

Cart and Shopping Flow

Cart drawers, status announcements, focus behavior, and the path from product selection toward checkout, where the store configuration allows review.

Code-Level Fixes and Retesting

Remediation in the agreed theme code and retesting of the affected keyboard and screen-reader interactions.

Shopify Accessibility Work, Scoped to Your Store

Depending on the project, work may include a manual audit, prioritized findings, fixes in Shopify Liquid, HTML, CSS, or JavaScript, and retesting. Third-party apps and checkout customization options vary, so their review and remediation need to be confirmed during scoping.

  1. Choose the scope: agree on the key templates, components, and shopping tasks to review.
  2. Test manually: check keyboard use, focus, forms, and representative screen-reader interactions alongside automated checks.
  3. Agree the quote: receive a written scope, deliverables, and fixed project quote before paid work starts.
  4. Fix and retest: implement the agreed changes and retest the affected interactions.

Shopify Accessibility Questions

Can you audit Shopify Plus stores?

Yes. Shopify and Shopify Plus storefronts can be reviewed. The exact scope depends on the theme, apps, checkout setup, and the access available for the project.

Can you fix checkout accessibility?

I can review the checkout journey and address issues where the store configuration allows. Available changes depend on the Shopify plan, checkout extensions, and third-party tools involved; I confirm feasibility before quoting.

Does a Shopify accessibility audit guarantee ADA compliance?

No. An audit and remediation project can test and address agreed WCAG criteria, but it is not legal advice or a guarantee of legal compliance. Consult qualified counsel about legal obligations.

Can I start with a small project?

Yes. Start with a focused review of important templates or a key shopping journey. The scope and fee are agreed before paid work begins.
