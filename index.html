Frequently Asked Questions

Which regional accessibility requirements do you support?

I support WCAG 2.2 work for ADA and Section 508 projects in the US, the EAA in Europe, AODA in Canada, and UK Equality Act / PSBAR expectations. The relevant standard and scope are agreed before work begins.

Can you implement the fixes as well as audit?

Yes. You can request a manual audit, code-level remediation, retesting, or a scoped combination. The deliverables are agreed before work starts.

Can you help if a full agency project is out of my budget?

Yes. Start with a focused review of your highest-priority pages or user journeys. I’ll quote the agreed work upfront, and you can decide whether to proceed or expand the scope later.
