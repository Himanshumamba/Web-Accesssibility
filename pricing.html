How I scope a project

The quote depends on the number of unique page templates, interactive components, key user journeys, testing environments, and whether code fixes or retesting are included. Repeated URLs that use the same template do not automatically count as separate full reviews.

Before work starts, you receive a written list of what is included, what is out of scope, the schedule, and the total fee. No ongoing contract is required for a one-time project.

Note: an audit reports on the agreed scope and WCAG criteria. It is not legal certification and cannot guarantee legal compliance.
