About Himanshu Pandey

I help businesses identify and fix website accessibility barriers through manual testing and practical, code-level remediation.

Platforms and technologies I support

I work across content management systems, ecommerce platforms, website builders, plugins, themes, and custom web applications.

WordPress & WooCommerce

Accessibility remediation across Elementor, Divi, Gutenberg, custom PHP themes, WooCommerce, and commonly used WordPress plugins and form builders.

Shopify & Shopify Plus

Accessibility improvements involving Liquid themes, navigation, cart drawers, product interfaces, forms, checkout experiences, and third-party app integrations.

Webflow & Wix

Accessibility fixes involving native elements, custom JavaScript, ARIA attributes, forms, page structure, navigation, and interactive components.

Squarespace & Tilda

Accessibility improvements using platform settings and, where appropriate, custom CSS and JavaScript to address focus states, contrast, structure, and assistive technology access.

Magento

Accessibility support for ecommerce interfaces including navigation, product pages, forms, shopping cart functionality, and checkout-related components.

Plugins & Third-Party Components

Accessibility review and remediation for plugins, widgets, form tools, popups, sliders, menus, ecommerce components, booking systems, and other third-party integrations.

Custom Frameworks

Accessibility testing and remediation for React, Next.js, Vue, JavaScript applications, and headless CMS implementations.
