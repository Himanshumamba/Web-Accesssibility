Ways I can support your team

Accessibility audits

Manual review of agreed page templates and user journeys, with screenshots, severity, user impact, and practical fix guidance.

Code-level remediation

Implementation support for HTML, CSS, JavaScript, ARIA, forms, keyboard behavior, focus states, and interactive components.

Retesting and handoff

Check agreed fixes and provide a clear status update your project manager and developers can use.

Work can be scoped as a project or a recurring block of support. If you need work delivered behind your agency brand, include that in your inquiry so we can agree on the handoff and client communication before work starts.

How the project works

  1. Share the site or product, platform, key pages, and the type of help you need.
  2. Receive a written scope, deliverables, schedule, and fixed project quote before work begins.
  3. Get findings or fixes with evidence and a handoff your team can use.

Quotes are based on unique page templates, components, user journeys, and remediation complexity—not just the total number of URLs. The review scope and applicable WCAG criteria are agreed before testing.
