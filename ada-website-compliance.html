Common ADA Website Issues

Forms That Cannot Be Used

Missing labels, unclear errors, inaccessible required fields, and broken checkout or lead forms.

Navigation Barriers

Menus and popups that do not work with keyboard navigation or screen readers.

Content Barriers

Missing alt text, poor heading order, low contrast text, and unclear link purpose.
