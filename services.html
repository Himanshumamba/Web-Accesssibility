How My Accessibility Process Works

  1. Discovery & Scope: We review your platform, target standard (WCAG 2.2 AA, ADA, Section 508, EAA, AODA, or UK regulations), and key user journeys.
  2. Manual + Assistive Tech Testing: I test your templates and interactive flows with keyboard-only navigation, NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and browser DevTools.
  3. Prioritized Findings Report: You receive clear, severity-ranked issues with screenshots, reproduction steps, and code-level recommendations.
  4. Remediation & Retesting: Either your team implements the fixes with my guidance, or I apply the code-level fixes directly and retest to confirm conformance.

Manual Audits vs. Automated Scanners vs. Overlays

Overlay Widgets

Add a floating toolbar but leave broken DOM semantics, keyboard traps, unlabeled forms, and checkout barriers untouched in the underlying source code.

Automated Scanners Alone

Helpful for catching syntax and contrast errors, but typically detect only 30% to 50% of WCAG criteria and miss real keyboard and screen reader usability blockers.

Manual Audit + Code Fixes

Tests real user journeys with assistive technology and fixes the actual HTML, CSS, JS, and ARIA—delivering lasting accessibility and SEO benefits.
